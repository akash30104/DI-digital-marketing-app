## 1. Overview

Digital Inertia ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy describes how the **DI Digital Marketing App** (the "Application" or "App"), available as an Android application and web platform, collects, uses, stores, and protects information from registered users, including Studio Founders, Social Media Managers, Video Editors, and Client Partners.

By accessing or using the DI Digital Marketing App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide video production workflow management, task assignment, deliverable approvals, and social media performance tracking.

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Credentials:** Full name, business email address, role designation (Founder, Social Media Manager, Video Editor, or Client Partner), and authentication passwords.
- **Client & Brand Information:** Client company/brand name, contact person details, and authorized review notes.
- **Video Production Metadata:** Video shoot titles, episode numbers, storage locations (e.g., hard disk names, folder paths), editing deadlines, cut types (Reels / Full Videos), and progress tracking percentages (0% – 100%).
- **Cloud Asset & Media Links:** External video preview links and Google Drive asset URLs submitted by video editors for review and approval.
- **Feedback & Revision Notes:** Text feedback, revision requests, timestamped comments, and approval greenlight decisions provided during the review cycle.

### B. Automatically Collected Information & Social Media Insights
- **Performance Analytics:** Public video metric insights (including view counts, likes, comments, and shares) fetched for published videos via authorized public APIs or manager entries.
- **Technical Log Data:** Device type, operating system version, app version, network connectivity status, and basic crash diagnostics required to maintain app reliability.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following operational purposes:
1. **User Authentication & Role-Based Access Control:** To authenticate team members and clients, ensuring users access only features and content appropriate to their designated role.
2. **Client Isolation:** To strictly partition client brand deliverables so that Client Partners only view and review video cuts belonging to their specific brand.
3. **Video Workflow & Task Management:** To notify video editors of assigned tasks, track real-time editing progress, and alert social media managers and founders when cuts reach 100% completion.
4. **Content Review & Publishing:** To coordinate review feedback, approve video deliverables, and track publishing schedules across social channels (e.g., YouTube, Instagram).
5. **App Maintenance & Security:** To diagnose technical errors, prevent unauthorized system access, and optimize application performance across Android and web.

---

## 4. Permissions Requested by the Android Application

To operate effectively, the DI Digital Marketing Android app requests standard Android permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to communicate with our secure database backend (Supabase), submit progress updates, and sync video status.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect online/offline connectivity to maintain reliable real-time data synchronization.
- `android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` *(where applicable on supported Android versions)*: Required if you choose to export reports or locally save video review previews.

We do **not** access your location, microphone, camera, contacts, or SMS without explicit additional permission.

## 6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your data:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All communication between the app and backend services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Role-Based Row-Level Security:** Database tables are configured with strict access controls ensuring editors only access editing tasks, clients only view their brand deliverables, and founders retain administrative oversight.
- **Client Partitioning:** Brand deliverables and publishing insights are isolated per client account.

## 7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide production management services.

### Requesting Account or Data Deletion
Users and clients have the right to request deletion of their account and associated video production records at any time.

To request data deletion:
- **Email:** Send an email to **support@digitalinertia.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request - [Your Name or Brand]"*.
- **Direct App Deletion:** Studio Founders can delete project deliverables, recordings, or client profiles directly from the app's Management and Settings screens.
- **Processing Time:** Data deletion requests submitted via email are verified and executed within 7 business days. All associated profile details, feedback logs, and task mappings will be permanently removed from active databases.
