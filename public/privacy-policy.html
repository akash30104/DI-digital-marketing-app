Privacy Policy

Application: DI Digital Marketing App Developer: Digital Inertia Effective: November 9, 2026

Overview & Commitment

Digital Inertia ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how the DI Digital Marketing App (Android mobile app and web portal) collects, manages, uses, and protects information for our team members (Founders, Social Media Managers, Video Editors) and verified Client Partners.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide video production tracking, deliverable approvals, and social media performance insights:

  • Account Information: Name, business email address, role designation (Founder, Social Media Manager, Video Editor, or Client Partner), and encrypted credentials.
  • Client Brand Profiles: Brand/company names, authorized representatives, and dedicated review permissions.
  • Video Production Records: Episode titles, hard disk names, raw footage paths, editing progress percentages (0% to 100%), delivery deadlines, and cut classifications (Reels vs. Full Videos).
  • Media & Cloud URLs: Google Drive links, review URLs, and Frame.io references submitted by editors for approval.
  • Feedback & Approval Logs: Timestamped review notes, requested revisions, greenlight approvals, and publishing schedules.
  • Public Performance Metrics: View counts, likes, comments, and shares retrieved via official public APIs for published video reels.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to power the production workflow:

  • Role-Based Access: To restrict access so Video Editors only see assigned tasks, Social Media Managers manage publishing pipelines, Founders maintain administrative oversight, and Client Partners review solely their own brand's cuts.
  • Client Isolation: To ensure complete data isolation where each client brand only accesses their specific deliverables and published insights.
  • Workflow Progression: To track progress updates (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and notify managers when video assets are ready for review.
  • Operational Security: To maintain session security, prevent unauthorized account access, and ensure high system availability.

3. Device & Android Permissions

The Android version of the DI Digital Marketing App requests minimal, transparent permissions:

Permission Platform Operational Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Android Required for real-time synchronization with our encrypted database, video status updates, and authentication.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Android Detects network availability to cache edits and prevent data loss during offline periods.

We do not access your physical location, camera, microphone, contact list, or SMS messages.

We do not sell, lease, or monetize your personal or brand information to third-party ad networks or brokers.

5. Client Data Isolation & Security

We employ multi-tenant isolation protocols:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All web traffic and mobile API calls require HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Strict Partitioning: Each client account is bound to its unique brand identity. Deliverables, review notes, and performance stats of one client are completely inaccessible to other clients.
  • Role Boundaries: Video editors cannot access financial records or client billing settings.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain full control over your information:

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your profile, brand assets, and video records at any time:

  • By Email: Contact us at support@digitalinertia.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - [Your Name / Brand]".
  • Directly in App: Studio Founders can delete deliverables, recordings, or user profiles from the App Management settings.

All verified deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, resulting in complete removal from production databases.

7. Children's Privacy

The DI Digital Marketing App is a professional marketing and video production tool designed for commercial businesses and adults. It is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal details from children.
